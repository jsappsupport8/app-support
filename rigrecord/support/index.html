RigRecord Support

Answers to common questions about your RV maintenance records, backups, reminders, reports, and RigRecord Pro.

About RigRecord

RigRecord is an RV maintenance log for tracking service records, mileage, generator hours, maintenance schedules, reminders, photos, receipts, costs, and maintenance reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my RigRecord data stored?

RigRecord data is stored locally on your device. RigRecord does not automatically upload vehicle or maintenance records to a developer-operated server.

Create backups when needed so you can retain or move your records.

How do I back up my data?

Use RigRecord's Backup function from Data Management. A backup may contain vehicle information, maintenance history, photos, receipts, settings, and other app data.

Keep backup files safe. If you save a backup to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or another service, that service's privacy and security policies apply.

How do I restore a backup?

Use RigRecord's Restore function and select a valid .rvjournalbackup file. A backup containing Pro-only data may require RigRecord Pro before it can be restored.

When appropriate, create a current backup before performing a major restore operation.

How do maintenance reminders work?

Maintenance Reminders are enabled by default for new users. The default timing is seven days before scheduled date-based maintenance, at approximately 9:00 AM in the device's local time on the reminder date.

You can change Notify Before in Settings. Notification permission must also be enabled in iOS. Delivery is not guaranteed and can be affected by device settings, Focus modes, device state, iOS behavior, and your configuration. Do not rely on a reminder as your only way to track required maintenance.

Mileage and generator-hour reminders depend on values you enter because RigRecord cannot automatically read your RV's mileage or generator hours.

How do I request a refund?

RigRecord Pro purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its applicable terms and policies.

Use Apple's official Report a Problem service to review available refund options. The RigRecord developer does not directly process payment-card refunds.

How do I restore RigRecord Pro?

RigRecord Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. There is no subscription.

Choose Restore Purchases from the RigRecord Pro purchase screen. Purchase entitlement is handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

What is included with RigRecord Pro?

  • Full Statistics
  • Professional Maintenance Reports
  • Multiple RVs
  • Receipts & Evidence
  • Custom Maintenance
  • Custom Tags, where applicable

How does the free Annual Report work?

After you build the required initial service history, you can generate one Annual Report for free. The free report is consumed only after successful PDF generation.

RigRecord Pro unlocks ongoing report generation and additional report types.

How do I create or share a maintenance report?

RigRecord generates reports locally as PDF files. You choose where to save or share them using Apple's system Share Sheet. RigRecord does not automatically upload reports to the developer.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete supported records and vehicle information using the available controls in RigRecord. RigRecord has no developer account or server containing your maintenance database.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data. Create a backup first if you want to retain your records. Backup files, PDFs, and other copies you previously exported must be deleted separately from Files, cloud storage, email, or any other location where you saved them.
