About RigRecord

RigRecord is a recordkeeping and organizational tool for RV owners. It helps you record, track, remind, summarize, and export vehicle and maintenance information.

RigRecord is not an RV manufacturer, repair shop, mechanic, inspection service, diagnostic service, or safety evaluation service. It does not inspect your vehicle, determine its mechanical condition, or determine what maintenance a particular RV requires.

Maintenance Information

Maintenance intervals, due dates, Due Soon and Overdue indicators, and reminder dates are calculated from settings and information entered by the user. RigRecord does not automatically determine the manufacturer’s maintenance schedule for your RV or its components.

The preconfigured maintenance intervals in RigRecord are default input values provided for convenience. They are not recommended maintenance intervals for any specific RV. Review and set the appropriate maintenance intervals for your vehicle and components, and always follow the applicable manufacturer documentation.

Always prioritize the RV manufacturer’s owner documentation, manufacturer service information, component manufacturer instructions, and applicable recalls or service information. Maintenance needs can vary by vehicle, equipment, operating environment, mileage, load, and driving conditions.

Safety-Critical Maintenance

Use qualified professional assistance and applicable manufacturer documentation when appropriate, especially for brakes, tires, steering, suspension, propane or LP systems, electrical systems, fuel systems, and other safety-critical components.

Do not treat a RigRecord entry, status, or calculation as a professional inspection, diagnosis, repair recommendation, or safety evaluation.

Maintenance Reminders

Maintenance reminders are a convenience feature and are not guaranteed to be delivered. Delivery can be affected by iOS notification permission, device settings, Focus modes, device state, operating-system behavior, and user configuration.

Do not rely on RigRecord reminders as your only method of tracking required maintenance. Mileage and generator hours are not read automatically from your RV and depend on values you enter.

Statistics and Reports

Statistics and PDF reports summarize information entered or stored in RigRecord. Their accuracy depends on the completeness and accuracy of the underlying records.

Reports are not manufacturer-certified maintenance records, dealer or repair-shop certifications, inspection certificates, warranty certifications, vehicle valuations, accounting records, or guarantees of vehicle condition. The developer does not review, verify, or certify their contents.

Data, Backups, and Exports

RigRecord generally stores user data on the device. The developer does not keep a server-side copy of your maintenance database and cannot remotely access or recover it.

You are responsible for creating backups of important data and keeping exported .rvjournalbackup files and PDF reports in a safe location. Data can be lost because of device loss or failure, device reset, app deletion, accidental deletion, file corruption, storage problems, or other technical causes.

When you export or share a backup or PDF using Files, Share Sheet, or another user-selected destination, you are responsible for the exported file and selected destination. The privacy and security policies of any third-party service you choose may apply.

The developer does not control files after they are exported or shared.
