RigRecord Privacy Policy

RigRecord is designed to keep RV maintenance information on your device.

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Overview

RigRecord is designed to keep RV maintenance information on the user's device.

The developer does not operate a server that automatically receives the user's vehicle records, maintenance history, photos, receipts, mileage, generator hours, notes, or generated reports.

Data We Collect

The developer does not collect personal data or RV maintenance data through RigRecord v1.0.

  • No analytics SDK
  • No advertising SDK
  • No tracking SDK
  • No developer-operated telemetry
  • No developer-operated user account
  • No developer-operated cloud database

Apple may process information when providing App Store and StoreKit services, as described below. That Apple processing is separate from collection by the RigRecord developer.

Data Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use RigRecord, the following information may be stored locally:

  • Vehicle information
  • Maintenance items and schedules
  • Service records
  • Mileage and generator hours
  • Costs, parts, shop information, and notes
  • Tags
  • Work photos and receipts
  • Reminder settings
  • Statistics-related source data
  • Report state
  • Backup-related settings

These data are used locally to provide RigRecord functionality.

Photos and Camera

RigRecord accesses photos only when the user selects them. Camera access occurs only when the user chooses to capture an image.

Selected or captured images are used for RigRecord records and are not automatically uploaded to a developer server.

Notifications

RigRecord uses local notifications for maintenance reminders. Reminder scheduling is performed on the device.

The developer does not operate a notification server for maintenance reminders.

Purchases

RigRecord Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer does not receive payment-card information through RigRecord.

Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple's own privacy policies.

Backups

Users may create .rvjournalbackup files. Backups may contain vehicle data, maintenance history, photos, receipts, settings, and other RigRecord data.

RigRecord does not automatically upload backup files to the developer. Users choose where backups are stored and should keep them in a safe place.

PDF Reports and Sharing

Reports are generated locally. Users choose whether and where to share or save them.

Sharing through Files, iCloud Drive, email, Google Drive, Dropbox, or another service is initiated by the user. Once data is shared with another service, that service's privacy policy applies.

Third-Party Services

RigRecord v1.0 does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Apple system services such as StoreKit, Photos Picker, camera permissions, Files, Share Sheet, and Local Notifications may be used as part of iOS functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

RigRecord data remains on the user's device until it is modified or deleted by the user, or removed with the app or system storage.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete the user's local RigRecord database because no developer-operated account or server stores it.

Exported backup and PDF copies must be deleted separately by the user from wherever they were saved.

Children's Privacy

RigRecord does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer-operated service because the app does not transmit such information to the developer.

Security

RigRecord relies on iOS application sandboxing and Apple platform security for local app storage. No system can provide absolute security.

Backup and PDF files exported by the user may exist outside the app sandbox and should be stored securely.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when RigRecord functionality or data practices change.

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, developer support uploads, advertising, tracking, or server notifications, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed and updated.
